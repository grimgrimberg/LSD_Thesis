Interpretation boundary

This makes the proposal stronger: the audience sees exactly which claims are supported, proxy-supported, exploratory, or blocked.

Supported now

The repo can defend a macro-dynamics surrogate, an empirical LSD anchor, uncertainty gates, and subject-disjoint benchmark framing.

  • ds006072 psilocybin external validation
  • Motion/confound control result
Proxy-supported

Transition, control, metastability, and structural-connectome language is model-level and engineering-inspired unless external biological data closes the gate.

  • Control/energy language is an explanatory prior.
  • DTI/SC layers are anatomical coupling priors, not raw scan viewers.
  • 8-module summaries are communication layers, not final anatomical inference.
Exploratory

Receptor, myelin, functional-gradient, and gene-expression overlays are useful hypothesis generators only.

  • Module-level map alignment is shown with uncertainty caveats.
  • Spatial-null neuromaps upgrades remain the stricter inference target.
  • Negative alignment results are preserved instead of hidden.
Blocked / future work

A strong mechanism claim is downgraded if motion controls, Schaefer/Yeo spatial nulls, psilocybin replication, or subject-disjoint ML fail.

  • Schaefer/Yeo high-resolution parcellation layer
  • Full neuromaps spatial-autocorrelation nulls
  • Receptor/myelin/gradient claim resolution
  • Project phase
