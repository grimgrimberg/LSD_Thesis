Likely questions

Conservative answers

What is the thesis claim?

A transparent macro-dynamic surrogate workbench can rank candidate mechanism proxies against paired LSD/placebo summary evidence, with explicit gates.

Does this prove an LSD mechanism?

No. It supports model-level ranking. It does not prove receptor biology, clinical effects, or subjective effects.

Why is C strongest?

C has the highest current support score and strongest bootstrap rank-1 fraction, but still needs motion-sensitive and null review.

Why keep B?

B is a sanity baseline. Keeping it visible prevents cherry-picking and preserves negative evidence.
