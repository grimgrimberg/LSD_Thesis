# PI Pitch: AI, Engineering, Perception, and Psychedelic-State Dynamics

## Falsifiable thesis claim

This project tests whether empirical LSD-placebo macro-dynamics are better explained by altered transition/control dynamics than by generic noise, motion, run effects, or static connectivity changes.

## Short PI-facing pitch

I am not pitching myself as a finished neuroscientist. I am pitching an AI and engineering project that uses psychedelic neuroimaging as a high-signal perturbation of perception. The core contribution is a reproducible research system: data ingestion, dynamic feature extraction, leak-proof ML validation, external-prior alignment, uncertainty gates, and a public dashboard that exposes both positive and negative evidence.

## Why this belongs in a Master's project

- It combines AI, engineering, perception, and psychedelics without pretending to solve all of neuroscience.
- It turns complex human neuroimaging into falsifiable computational claims.
- It makes uncertainty visible instead of hiding weak results.
- It creates a platform that can grow into stronger neuroscience through Schaefer/Yeo parcellations, neuromaps spatial nulls, and authorized external psilocybin datasets.

## Core presentation structure

1. Motivation: psychedelic states as perturbations of perception and integration.
2. Engineering contribution: reproducible dynamic-analysis and dashboard system.
3. Empirical anchor: ds003059 LSD-placebo macro-dynamics.
4. AI benchmark: subject-disjoint condition prediction, no window-random leakage.
5. Controls: motion, run/music, preprocessing, signal quality, and null models.
6. External priors: receptor, DTI/structural-connectome, myelin, gradient, and AHBA maps.
7. Honest negative result: current receptor/myelin/gradient alignments are not strong enough for mechanism claims.
8. Roadmap: Schaefer/Yeo high-resolution inference and neuromaps spatial nulls.
