Data handling

Dashboarding

The live dashboard is represented here through offline screenshots and copied tables. No server is needed to review this package.

Method object. The model is an 8-module stochastic graph surrogate with latent states, adaptation, graph coupling, hierarchy constraints, stochastic perturbation, and macro-level proxy metrics. These are modeling choices for ranking, not direct biological parameters.

A-E layers

A transition-state proxy; B DMDc baseline; C hierarchy/routing; D dynamic repertoire; E finite-horizon network-control energy proxy.

Validation posture

Internal bootstrap and CV5 bookkeeping evaluate robustness. They do not replace motion/confound proof, cross-dataset stress-test evidence, or PI approval.
