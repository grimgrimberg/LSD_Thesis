# Claim Ladder for PI Pitch

## One-sentence pitch

This project uses AI, control theory, and interactive visualization to study how psychedelic-state datasets can be turned into testable macro-dynamic models of perception, while keeping biological claims conservative and falsifiable.

## Why this is a Master's-level fit

- The project is not trying to become a full neuroscience thesis overnight.
- The contribution is an engineering and AI framework: data ingestion, subject-disjoint validation, dynamic feature extraction, model comparison, uncertainty gating, and a transparent dashboard.
- Psychedelic neuroimaging is the motivating domain because it creates unusually strong perturbations of perception, integration, uncertainty, and state transitions.
- The PI-facing value is a student who can build reproducible computational infrastructure around difficult multimodal human data.

## Claim tiers

| Tier | Claim | Current status | What makes it acceptable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | The project can build a reproducible dashboard around empirical LSD-placebo macro-dynamics. | Supported | Source code, generated status artifacts, static dashboard, and explicit provenance are implemented. |
| B | LSD-placebo differences can be summarized as macro-dynamic changes in transition, integration, and control-like metrics. | Supported as empirical proxy | The claim stays at module/dynamics level and does not claim subjective experience or receptor causality. |
| C | AI/ML benchmarks can test whether dynamic windows carry condition information without window-random leakage. | Supported internally | Subject-disjoint CV and no window-random reporting are the required standard. |
| D | Receptor, DTI, myelin, functional-gradient, and transcriptomic maps provide useful priors for interpretation. | Exploratory | Current alignments are shown with p/q/CI gates and are not presented as proof. |
| E | A strong receptor/myelin/gradient mechanism is established. | Not supported yet | Requires higher-resolution Schaefer/Yeo or Glasser alignment, spatial nulls, and external replication. |
| F | The project externally validates LSD dynamics in psilocybin datasets. | Blocked / future work | Requires authorized comparable data and unchanged scoring. Current Lyons/PsiConnect references are context, not validation. |

## What should be emphasized to a PI

- This is an AI and engineering project using neuroscience as a high-value test bed.
- The dashboard is a research operating system: it exposes evidence, uncertainty, failures, and next experiments.
- Negative or weak biological alignments are not a failure; they are exactly what a rigorous claim-gating system should show.
- The project can grow toward real neuroscience by adding Schaefer/Yeo parcel-level extraction, neuromaps spatial nulls, and authorized external psilocybin datasets.

## What should not be claimed

- Do not claim the 8 macro modules are canonical brain networks.
- Do not claim receptor, DTI, myelin, gradient, or AHBA alignment proves mechanism.
- Do not claim the model simulates subjective psychedelic experience.
- Do not claim clinical validation or therapeutic prediction.
- Do not claim psilocybin replication until comparable data are ingested and scored unchanged.

## Falsification standards

The mechanism story should be downgraded if:

- effects disappear under subject-disjoint validation,
- effects disappear after motion/run/music controls,
- Schaefer/Yeo parcel-level results do not survive spatial nulls,
- external psilocybin datasets fail under unchanged scoring,
- receptor/gradient/myelin priors remain weaker than random, degree, or uniform controls.

## Next 90-day PI-facing plan

1. Freeze the current 8-module layer as an interpretable macro-summary.
2. Add Schaefer-100 or Schaefer-200 with Yeo-7/Yeo-17 labels as the primary inference layer.
3. Project receptor, myelin, functional-gradient, and AHBA maps to the same parcel space.
4. Run neuromaps-style spatial nulls and report q-values plus CI-zero gates.
5. Add a leak-proof ML benchmark summary that only reports subject-disjoint performance.
6. Prepare authorized external psilocybin scoring as future validation, not as a current claim.
