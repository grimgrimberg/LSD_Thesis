Research Controls and Context

Use these panels as a thesis evidence console: inspect the model, empirical summaries, CV5 internal-validation status, provenance, and generated artifacts. The fMRI previews are downsampled exploratory displays, not diagnostic anatomical images.

Scientific Story

This dashboard accompanies a transparent surrogate model of altered-state-inspired macro-dynamics. The working question is whether explicit control-theoretic proxy dynamics can be ranked against cached empirical targets without claiming receptor-level or subjective realism.

  • Modeled: transition/barrier dynamics and controlled linear dynamics in a coarse 8-module proxy space.
  • Compared: cached ds003059 placebo/LSD summaries, literature-style targets, model deltas, and ranking baselines.
  • Validated: preliminary internal subject-disjoint CV5 checks on held-out subjects within the same dataset.
  • Not validated: external cohorts, clinical use, receptor pharmacology, or subjective LSD state.

Dynamic Method Plan

Approved research path: fit transition/barrier dynamics and DMDc first, then use hierarchy and repertoire metrics as explanation and robustness layers.

  • A: state occupancy, dwell, switching, and transition-effort proxy ranking.
  • B: controlled linear dynamics with condition or mechanism inputs.
  • C/D: hierarchy, precision-relaxation, integration, segregation, and dynamic FC checks.
  • MPC-like scoring stays future-facing until A+B are stable and mathematically justified.

How to Read This Dashboard

Read model, empirical, and literature-style deltas by direction, uncertainty, and missingness. Treat the plots as exploratory inspection panels, not proof surfaces.

Validation badges distinguish seed-sampling robustness from subject-disjoint internal validation. Missing artifacts are reported as pending outputs, not as inferred results.

Current QC exposure is aggregate-only; it does not include subject-level motion, FD/DVARS, confound, or censoring strata.
